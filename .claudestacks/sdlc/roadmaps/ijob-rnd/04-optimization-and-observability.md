# Milestone: Optimization and observability

## Goal

GEPA and other DSPy optimizers tune a cheap model from the labels under a budget cap, and a report compares it with a frontier baseline on held-out postings. Traces, DeepEval scores and optimizer runs are recorded in a local Arize Phoenix, ready to show as evidence of the results.

## Non-goals

- Committing personal tuned programs or labels.
- A hosted observability service.
