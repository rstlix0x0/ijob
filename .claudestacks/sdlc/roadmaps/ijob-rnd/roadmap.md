# Roadmap: ijob R&D

## Vision

ijob is a tool people use to find jobs in any field, and an R&D project and portfolio piece on
building agentic systems and apps with LLMs. It answers fast, ranks postings against a CV, measures
its LLM steps, optimizes cheap models, records the results and adds agents. Each milestone ends with
a result shown on real data.

## Non-goals

- Bypassing anti-bot protection, logged-in scraping or applying to jobs automatically.
